\section*{Emergency Warning Signs}
\label{sec:quickref_emergency}

Seek immediate medical attention if you experience any of the following:

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item Chest pain or pressure, especially with shortness of breath
  \item Sudden severe headache (``worst headache of my life'')
  \item Sudden weakness or numbness on one side of the body
  \item Sudden difficulty speaking or understanding speech
  \item Sudden vision loss or double vision
  \item Difficulty breathing or choking
  \item Uncontrolled bleeding
  \item High fever with stiff neck and confusion
  \item Seizure (especially first-time or prolonged)
  \item Loss of consciousness or fainting
  \item Severe abdominal pain with vomiting or fever
  \item Suicidal thoughts or intent
  \item Severe allergic reaction (swelling, hives, difficulty breathing)
  \item Head or spine injury with any neurological symptoms
  \item Poisoning or overdose
\end{itemize}
