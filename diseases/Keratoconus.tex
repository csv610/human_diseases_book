\chapter{Keratoconus}
\index{Keratoconus}
\label{sec:Keratoconus}

\section{Overview}


\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item Keratoconus is a progressive, non-inflammatory ectatic disorder of the cornea characterized by progressive thinning and steepening, resulting in irregular astigmatism and reduced visual acuity
  \item Onset is typically in adolescence or early adulthood.
\end{itemize}
\section{Causes}
This condition happens because of structural weakening of the corneal stroma. The underlying mechanisms involve complex interactions between genetic predisposition and environmental factors that disrupt normal physiological processes. Disruption of normal physiological processes leads to the characteristic features of the condition. Multiple contributing factors may act synergistically to trigger or worsen the condition. Understanding the specific cause helps guide targeted treatment approaches.
\section{Risk Factors}


\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item Genetic predisposition and family history
  \item Advancing age
  \item Lifestyle factors (diet, exercise, smoking, alcohol)
  \item Environmental exposures
  \item Underlying medical conditions
  \item Medication use
\end{itemize}
\section{Signs and Symptoms}

\subsection{Common symptoms}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item You may experience progressive visual distortion and decreased visual acuity
  \item Slit-lamp findings include Munson's sign, Vogt's striae, and corneal iron deposition.
  \item Pain or discomfort in the affected area
  \end{itemize}

\subsection{Emergency warning signs}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item Severe or worsening symptoms of keratoconus require immediate medical evaluation
\end{itemize}
\section{Progression and Stages}
The condition may progress through different stages of severity over time. Early stages often involve mild or subtle symptoms that may be overlooked. Moderate stages involve more noticeable symptoms affecting daily function. Advanced stages may involve significant impairment and complications.

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item Management progresses from spectacles and rigid gas-permeable contact lenses to corneal crosslinking to halt progression, intrastromal corneal ring segments, and ultimately keratoplasty in advanced cases. The outlook varies from person to person
  \item Corneal crosslinking effectively halts progression.
\end{itemize}
\section{Complications}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item Disease progression leading to worsening symptoms
  \item Secondary infections or injuries
  \item Side effects of treatment
  \item Reduced quality of life
  \item Emotional and psychological impact
\end{itemize}
