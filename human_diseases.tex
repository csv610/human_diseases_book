\chapter*{Preface}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface}

\noindent
All About Human Diseases is a comprehensive, patient-oriented medical reference
that catalogs 891 diseases and disorders affecting every organ and system of
the human body. Each chapter follows a consistent 11-section structure designed
to give readers a complete yet accessible understanding of each condition:
Overview, Causes, Risk Factors, Signs and Symptoms, Progression and Stages,
Complications, Diagnosis, Prevention, Treatment Options, Living with It,
and Outlook.

\vspace{12pt}
\noindent
This book is intended for students, educators, healthcare trainees, and anyone
seeking to understand diseases in plain, jargon-free language. Medical
terminology has been simplified throughout without sacrificing accuracy, making
complex medical concepts approachable for a general audience.

\vspace{12pt}
\noindent
The Organ--Disease Dictionary appendix provides an alternative navigation
path, grouping every disease by the primary organ or organ system it affects.
A comprehensive index with page references completes the volume.

\vspace{12pt}
\noindent
\emph{Chaman Singh Verma}

\chapter*{Foreword}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Foreword}

\noindent
The study of human disease is the study of human life itself. Each of the
891 entries in this volume represents not only a clinical entity but a
story---of how the body responds to injury, infection, genetic mutation, and
the wear of time. This book brings those stories together in a format that
prioritizes clarity and compassion.

\vspace{12pt}
\noindent
Medicine advances daily, but the fundamental need to understand what happens
to our bodies and why remains constant. Whether you are a student beginning
your medical journey, a practitioner refreshing your knowledge, or a patient
or caregiver seeking reliable information, I hope this reference serves you
well.
