% Medical Glossary
\chapter*{Glossary of Medical Terms}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Glossary of Medical Terms}

\begin{description}[font=\normalfont\bfseries,leftmargin=3em,labelwidth=2.5em]
\item[Aneurysm] Abnormal bulging or weakening of a blood vessel wall
\item[Biopsy] Removal of tissue sample for microscopic examination
\item[Carcinoma] Malignant tumor arising from epithelial tissue
\item[Cause] The agent, factor, or mechanism producing a disease
\item[Chronic] Long-standing or persistent condition (duration >3 months)
\item[Clinical] Related to patient observation, examination, and diagnosis
\item[Complication] Secondary disease or condition developing during course of another
\item[Cortex] Outer layer of an organ or structure (e.g., renal cortex)
\item[Curable] Capable of being completely treated or healed
\item[Diagnosis] Identification of disease through signs, symptoms, and testing
\item[Etiology] Study of causes or origin of disease
\item[Granuloma] Small area of inflammation formed by immune cells (macrophages)
\item[Hematopoiesis] Formation and development of blood cellular components
\item[Hyperplasia] Increase in number of cells in an organ or tissue
\item[Hypertrophy] Enlargement of organ or tissue from cell growth
\item[Idiopathic] Disease of unknown cause
\item[Immunodeficiency] Inadequate immune response to pathogens
\item[Inflammation] Body's protective response to injury or infection
\item[Infection] Invasion and multiplication of pathogenic microorganisms
\item[Lesion] Area of abnormal tissue change from disease or injury
\item[Malignant] Cancerous; capable of invasion and metastasis
\item[Marrow] Soft tissue inside bones producing blood cells (bone marrow)
\item[Mucosa] Membrane lining body cavities open to exterior
\item[Mutations] Changes in DNA sequence that may cause disease
\item[Necrosis] Death of cells or tissue due to injury or disease
\item[Neoplasm] Abnormal new growth of tissue (tumor)
\item[Nodule] Small knot-like mass or lump in tissue
\item[Obstruction] Blockage of a body passage or canal
\item[Organism] Living microorganism (bacterium, virus, fungus, etc.)
\item[Pathogen] Disease-causing microorganism
\item[Pathophysiology] Abnormal function associated with disease process
\item[Perforation] Creation of hole in tissue or organ wall
\item[Polyuria] Excessive urine production (>2.5 L/day)
\item[Prognosis] Predicted course and outcome of disease
\item[Protector] Substance or factor providing defense against disease
\item[Remission] Temporary decrease or disappearance of disease symptoms
\item[Sarcoma] Malignant tumor arising from connective tissue
\item[Sclerosis] Abnormal hardening of tissue (e.g., atherosclerosis)
\item[Symptom] Subjective evidence of disease perceived by patient
\item[Therapy] Treatment of disease to cure or relieve symptoms
\item[Tumor] Abnormal mass of tissue (neoplasm)
\item[Vasculitis] Inflammation of blood vessels
\item[Acute] Sudden onset, severe, short duration
\item[Benign] Non-cancerous; not invasive or metastatic
\item[Biochemical] Related to chemical processes in living organisms
\item[Constitutional] Relating to the entire body or general health
\item[Dehydration] Deficiency of body water
\item[Embolism] Blockage of a blood vessel by a clot or foreign material
\item[Focal] Limited to one area or region
\item[Gastrointestinal] Relating to stomach and intestines
\item[Hemorrhage] Loss of blood from vessels
\item[Hyperglycemia] Excess glucose in blood
\item[Hypoglycemia] Deficient glucose in blood
\item[Ischemia] Inadequate blood supply to tissue
\item[Metastasis] Spread of cancer to distant sites
\item[Mucous membrane] Moist tissue lining body cavities
\item[Nephrotoxic] Poisonous to kidneys
\item[Neurotoxic] Poisonous to nervous system
\item[Oncology] Study of tumors and cancer
\item[Physiology] Normal function of living organisms
\item[Prophylaxis] Prevention of disease
\item[Thrombosis] Formation of blood clot in vessel
\item[Toxicology] Study of poisons and their effects
\item[Ulcer] Open sore on skin or mucous membrane
\item[Viral] Caused by virus
\item[Autoimmune] Immune response against own tissues
\item[Bacterial] Caused by bacteria
\item[Fungal] Caused by fungus
\item[Parasitic] Caused by parasite
\item[Malignancy] Presence of cancer
\item[Inflammation] Localized response to injury
\item[Infection] Invasion by pathogens
\item[Immunology] Study of immune system
\item[Cardiology] Study of heart
\item[Neurology] Study of nervous system
\item[Gastroenterology] Study of digestive system
\item[Endocrinology] Study of endocrine glands
\item[Dermatology] Study of skin
\item[Hematology] Study of blood
\item[Oncology] Study of cancer
\item[Radiology] Study of medical imaging
\item[Surgery] Medical procedure involving operation
\item[Anatomy] Study of body structure
\item[Pathology] Study of disease processes
\end{description}
