\chapter*{Glossary of Medical Terms}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Glossary of Medical Terms}

\begin{description}[font=\normalfont\bfseries,leftmargin=3em,labelwidth=2.5em]
\item[Aneurysm] Abnormal bulging or weakening of a blood vessel wall
\item[Biopsy] Removal of tissue sample for examination
\item[Carcinoma] Cancer arising from epithelial tissue
\item[Cause] The agent or factor producing a disease
\item[Chronic] Long-standing or persistent condition
\item[Clinical] Related to patient observation and examination
\item[Complication] Secondary disease or condition developing during course of another
\item[Cortex] Outer layer of an organ or structure
\item[Curable] Capable of being completely treated or healed
\item[Diagnosis] Identification of disease through examination
\item[Etiology] Study of causes or origin of disease
\item[Granuloma] Small area of inflammation formed by immune cells
\item[Hematopoiesis] Formation of blood cellular components
\item[Hyperplasia] Increase in number of cells in an organ
\item[Hypertrophy] Enlargement of organ or tissue from cell growth
\item[Idiopathic] Disease of unknown cause
\item[Immunodeficiency] Inadequate immune response
\item[Inflammation] Body's response to injury or infection
\item[Infection] Invasion by pathogenic microorganisms
\item[Lesion] Area of abnormal tissue change
\item[Malignant] Cancerous; capable of invasion and metastasis
\item[Marrow] Soft tissue inside bones producing blood cells
\item[Mucosa] Membrane lining body cavities
\item[Mutations] Changes in DNA sequence
\item[Necrosis] Death of cells or tissue
\item[Neoplasm] Abnormal new growth of tissue
\item[Nodule] Small knot-like mass or lump
\item[Obstruction] Blockage of a passage
\item[Organism] Living microorganism
\item[Pathogen] Disease-causing microorganism
\item[Pathophysiology] Abnormal function associated with disease
\item[Perforation] Creation of hole in tissue or organ
\item[Polyuria] Excessive urine production
\item[Prognosis] Predicted course and outcome of disease
\item[Protector] Defense against disease
\item[Remission] Temporary decrease or消失 of disease symptoms
\item[Sarcoma] Cancer arising from connective tissue
\item[Sclerosis] Abnormal hardening of tissue
\item[Symptom] Subjective evidence of disease
\item[Therapy] Treatment of disease
\item[Tumor] Abnormal mass of tissue
\item[Vasculitis] Inflammation of blood vessels
\end{description}
